### Permissions boundary

`server.js:167-174` authenticates every private `/api` request, then mounts handlers without a route/action role check. The earlier unauthenticated `/api/inventory` and `/api/inventory-sync/central/overview` requests returned HTTP 401; this proves only the sign-in boundary. It does not prove warehouse, approver, finance, or administrator role limits. A role matrix and separate test accounts are needed before changing authorization rules.
